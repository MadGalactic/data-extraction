Welcome to
Riverbend Christian Preschool!

We are an Outreach Mission of  Riverbend Lutheran Church

We enable children to demonstrate achievement in their intellectual, physical and creative development in a Learn Through Play environment.

Registration for 2025/26:

  • Registration is now open for the 2025/26 school year! Find more information here.

  • We accept registrations throughout the year. If your child turns 3 after September, they may start later in the year. Apply to get on our “under-3 waitlist.”

  • Classroom Visits: We welcome visitors by appointment. Visits can be scheduled during regular class times during the school year, or at another time convenient for you that can be arranged with the administrator.
